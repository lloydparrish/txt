Privacy Policy for IdleDev Bot

Effective date: October 1, 2026

IdleDev Bot ("the Bot") is a private Discord application used to control and review a locally hosted software-development orchestration system.

Information the Bot Processes

The Bot may process information necessary to respond to Discord interactions and operate its development workflow, including:

- Discord user IDs;
- Discord server (guild) IDs;
- Discord channel IDs;
- Discord interaction and message IDs;
- product ideas, requests, feedback, approvals, rejections, and other text deliberately submitted to the Bot;
- local job, review, notification, and operational status information;
- screenshots, test results, and other development evidence deliberately presented through the Bot.

The Bot does not use the Message Content privileged intent and does not monitor ordinary Discord conversations.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Bot's functionality, including:

- authenticating authorized users;
- recording submitted development requests;
- displaying development status;
- recording product decisions and feedback;
- controlling approved local development-server operations;
- presenting testing and review evidence;
- maintaining reliable local job and notification state.

Automated Development Processing

When automated development functionality is enabled, information intentionally submitted for a development task may be processed by the locally operated development system and may be provided to OpenAI Codex through the operator's authenticated Codex client for software-development purposes.

The Bot does not sell user information or use it for advertising.

Storage

The Bot's operational data is stored primarily on the operator's local Windows computer.

The Discord bot token is stored in the Windows Credential Manager and is not stored in the application's source repository or operational database.

Development records may include local SQLite records, logs, request history, test results, and review evidence.

Sharing

Information is not sold.

Information may be transmitted to:

- Discord, as necessary to receive interactions and send responses;
- OpenAI Codex, when automated development functionality processes an intentionally submitted development request;
- local development tools and services operated by the Bot owner as necessary to perform requested development work.

The Bot does not intentionally provide Discord data to unrelated third parties.

Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate, audit, recover, or review development work.

Operational and development records may be deleted when they are no longer needed.

Data Deletion Requests

A user whose information has been processed by the Bot may request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting the Bot operator through the GitHub repository hosting this Privacy Policy.

Deletion requests will be handled subject to any information that must be retained for security, legal, or system-integrity purposes.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to limit access to Bot data. The Bot uses owner, server, and channel authorization checks and stores secrets separately from source code and ordinary application data.

Children

The Bot is not intended for use by children under the minimum age permitted by Discord.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's functionality changes. The current version will remain publicly available at this location.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, open an issue in the GitHub repository hosting this policy.
